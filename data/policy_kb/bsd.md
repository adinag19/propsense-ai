# Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD)

BSD is a stamp duty payable by the buyer on all property purchases in Singapore. It applies to all buyers regardless of citizenship, residency, or number of properties owned. BSD is paid before ABSD is computed, but both are due at the same time.

## Current BSD Rates for Residential Properties (effective 15 February 2023)

BSD is computed on the purchase price or market value of the property, whichever is higher.

| Purchase Price Band | BSD Rate |
|---|---|
| First SGD 180,000 | 1% |
| Next SGD 180,000 | 2% |
| Next SGD 640,000 | 3% |
| Next SGD 500,000 | 4% |
| Next SGD 1,500,000 | 5% |
| Amount exceeding SGD 3,000,000 | 6% |

## BSD Calculation Examples

### Example 1: HDB resale flat at SGD 550,000
- First SGD 180,000 @ 1%: SGD 1,800
- Next SGD 180,000 @ 2%: SGD 3,600
- Remaining SGD 190,000 @ 3%: SGD 5,700
- Total BSD: SGD 11,100 (effective rate: 2.02%)

### Example 2: Private condo at SGD 1,500,000
- First SGD 180,000 @ 1%: SGD 1,800
- Next SGD 180,000 @ 2%: SGD 3,600
- Next SGD 640,000 @ 3%: SGD 19,200
- Remaining SGD 500,000 @ 4%: SGD 20,000
- Total BSD: SGD 44,600 (effective rate: 2.97%)

### Example 3: Luxury condo at SGD 5,000,000
- First SGD 180,000 @ 1%: SGD 1,800
- Next SGD 180,000 @ 2%: SGD 3,600
- Next SGD 640,000 @ 3%: SGD 19,200
- Next SGD 500,000 @ 4%: SGD 20,000
- Next SGD 1,500,000 @ 5%: SGD 75,000
- Remaining SGD 2,000,000 @ 6%: SGD 120,000
- Total BSD: SGD 239,600 (effective rate: 4.79%)

## BSD for Non-Residential Properties

Different rates apply to commercial, industrial, and other non-residential properties:
- First SGD 180,000: 1%
- Next SGD 180,000: 2%
- Remaining amount: 3%
(No additional higher bands for non-residential)

## BSD Payment

- BSD is due within 14 days of signing the OTP or S&P agreement
- Payable to IRAS
- Can be paid from CPF OA (subject to CPF withdrawal limits) or cash
- BSD is payable even if the deal subsequently falls through (partial refund may apply)

## BSD and ABSD Combined Example

SC buying a SGD 1,500,000 private condo as their 2nd property:
- BSD: SGD 44,600
- ABSD (20% of SGD 1,500,000): SGD 300,000
- Total stamp duty: SGD 344,600 (paid in cash/CPF, not from loan)

## Historical Changes

Before 15 February 2023, the top BSD rate was 4% (for amounts above SGD 1,000,000). The February 2023 revision added the 5% and 6% bands, significantly increasing BSD for high-value properties.

Prior rates (before Feb 2023):
- First SGD 180,000: 1%
- Next SGD 180,000: 2%
- Next SGD 640,000: 3%
- Remaining amount: 4%

Always verify rates against IRAS website before transacting.
