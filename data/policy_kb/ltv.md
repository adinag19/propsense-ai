# Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limit

The LTV limit caps the maximum loan a financial institution may grant relative to the property's value (or purchase price, whichever is lower). It determines the minimum down payment required.

## Current LTV Limits for Bank Loans (Private Property and HDB)

### First Housing Loan
- LTV limit: 75%
- Minimum down payment: 25%
  - At least 5% must be paid in cash
  - The remaining 20% may be from CPF OA or cash

### Second Housing Loan (Outstanding Loan Exists)
- LTV limit: 45%
- Minimum down payment: 55%
  - At least 25% must be paid in cash
  - Remaining 30% may be from CPF OA or cash

### Third and Subsequent Housing Loans
- LTV limit: 35%
- Minimum down payment: 65%
  - At least 25% must be paid in cash

## Age and Tenure Conditions

If the loan tenure extends beyond the borrower's age of 65, or if the tenure exceeds 30 years, the LTV is reduced:
- First loan: reduced from 75% to 55% (minimum cash down payment: 10%)
- Second loan: reduced from 45% to 25%

Loan tenure is capped at 30 years for HDB loans (25 years for HDB concessionary loans) and 35 years for bank loans on private property.

## HDB Concessionary Loan LTV

HDB's own concessionary loan (2.6% interest) offers:
- LTV: up to 80% of purchase price or value, whichever is lower
- Minimum down payment: 20% (can be entirely from CPF OA — no mandatory cash component)
- Eligibility: Singapore Citizens only, income ceiling applies, no outstanding private property

## Counting Loans

A loan is "outstanding" if any borrower or co-borrower on the new application has an existing property loan, regardless of whether the property is in Singapore or overseas. The outstanding loan count includes:
- HDB flat loans
- Private property bank loans
- Mortgages on overseas properties

## Cash vs CPF for Down Payment

### First Property (LTV 75%)
- Mandatory cash: 5% of purchase price
- Optional CPF: up to 20% of purchase price
- Example (SGD 1,000,000 property): SGD 50,000 cash + SGD 200,000 CPF + SGD 750,000 loan

### Second Property (LTV 45%)
- Mandatory cash: 25% of purchase price
- Optional CPF: up to 30% of purchase price
- ABSD (SC 2nd property: 20%) must also be paid in cash/CPF, not from loan
- Example (SGD 1,500,000 condo): SGD 375,000 cash + SGD 450,000 CPF + SGD 675,000 loan + SGD 300,000 ABSD

## Valuation and LTV

LTV is applied on the lower of:
- Purchase price agreed in OTP/S&P
- Bank's independent valuation of the property

If valuation is below purchase price (known as "cash over valuation" or COV):
- The difference must be paid fully in cash
- LTV loan is computed on the lower valuation, not the purchase price

## LTV for Foreigners

LTV limits apply equally to foreigners, PRs, and citizens. The same first/second/third loan tiers apply. However, foreigners face 60% ABSD on top of the down payment requirement.

## Historical Changes
- Sep 2022: second loan LTV tightened from 45% to 45% (no change to LTV, but ABSD raised)
- Dec 2021: LTV remained at current levels; ABSD rates increased
- Jul 2018: no change to LTV framework

Always verify current LTV rules with MAS or your bank before transacting.
