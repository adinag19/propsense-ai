# Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD)

ABSD is a stamp duty payable on top of Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD) when purchasing residential property in Singapore. It applies to Singapore Citizens buying a second or subsequent property, all Permanent Residents, all Foreigners, and all entities.

## Current ABSD Rates (effective 27 April 2023)

### Singapore Citizens (SC)
- 1st residential property: 0%
- 2nd residential property: 20%
- 3rd and subsequent residential property: 30%

### Singapore Permanent Residents (PR)
- 1st residential property: 5%
- 2nd residential property: 30%
- 3rd and subsequent residential property: 35%

### Foreigners (non-SC, non-PR)
- Any residential property: 60%

### Entities (companies, trusts)
- Any residential property: 65%
- Housing developers: 35% (remittable on application subject to conditions)

## Key Rules

### ABSD is Not Loan-Financeable
ABSD must be paid in full within 14 days of the date of the Option to Purchase (OTP) or Sale and Purchase Agreement (S&P). It cannot be paid from a bank loan. It must come from cash or CPF Ordinary Account funds.

### Counting Properties
The number of residential properties owned is counted at the time of purchase, including properties held jointly. Partial interests count as ownership.

### Joint Purchases (Mixed Citizenship)
For joint purchases between a SC and a foreigner, the higher ABSD rate (foreigner rate) applies to the entire purchase price.
For joint purchases between a SC and a PR, the PR rate applies.

### Married Couples — SC Couples Buying First Home Together
A SC married to a SC buying their first residential property together: 0% ABSD, provided neither has previously owned residential property.
A SC married to a PR buying their first home: PR rate (5%) applies on the entire transaction.

### Remission for SC Married Couples Decoupling
A SC couple may apply for ABSD remission when selling one existing property and buying a replacement property, if both own only one property and the first property is sold within 6 months of purchasing the replacement.

## ABSD and HDB Flats
HDB flats are residential properties for ABSD counting purposes.
A SC who owns one HDB flat buying a private condo pays 20% ABSD (2nd property rate).
A PR who owns one HDB flat buying any property pays 30% ABSD (2nd property rate).

## ABSD Payment Mechanics
- Due: within 14 days of OTP exercise or S&P signing (whichever is earlier)
- Payable to: IRAS
- Payment methods: cash or CPF OA (CPF portion subject to CPF withdrawal limits)
- Late payment: 6% penalty per annum on unpaid ABSD

## Historical Context
ABSD was first introduced in December 2011. Rates have been revised multiple times:
- Dec 2011: first introduction
- Jan 2013: rates increased
- Jul 2018: significant increase (foreigners moved from 15% to 20%)
- Dec 2021: further increases
- Apr 2023: current rates (foreigners: 60%, entities: 65%)

Always verify effective_date before transacting. Policy rates are subject to government review.
