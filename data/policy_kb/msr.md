# Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR)

MSR limits the portion of a borrower's gross monthly income that can be used for mortgage repayments on HDB flats and Executive Condominiums (ECs). It applies on top of TDSR.

## Current MSR Threshold: 30%

Monthly mortgage repayments for the HDB flat or EC being purchased must not exceed 30% of the borrower's gross monthly income.

## Properties MSR Applies To

MSR applies when buying:
1. HDB flats (new BTO, resale, DBSS) — financed by HDB loan or bank loan
2. Executive Condominiums (ECs) — only for bank loans (not HDB loans, since ECs are private)

MSR does NOT apply to private condominiums or landed properties.

## MSR vs TDSR

For HDB and EC purchases, BOTH MSR and TDSR must be satisfied:
- MSR: monthly HDB/EC mortgage repayment ≤ 30% of gross monthly income
- TDSR: total monthly debt repayments ≤ 55% of gross monthly income

MSR is typically the binding constraint because 30% < 55%.

## MSR Calculation Example

Gross monthly income: SGD 8,000
MSR limit: 30% × 8,000 = SGD 2,400/month maximum mortgage repayment

At a 3.5% interest rate over 25 years:
Maximum loan ≈ SGD 2,400 × 151 (annuity factor) ≈ SGD 430,000

## Income Definition for MSR

Same rules as TDSR:
- Fixed salary: 100% of gross monthly salary
- Variable/commission income: 70% of 12-month average
- Rental income: 70% of gross rental
- Self-employment: 70% of 2-year average NOA

## MSR and Joint Borrowers

For joint applications, the combined gross monthly income of all borrowers is used, subject to lender assessment.

## HDB Loan vs Bank Loan — MSR Interaction

### HDB Concessionary Loan
- Interest rate: 0.1% above CPF OA rate (currently ~2.6%)
- MSR applies: monthly repayment must not exceed 30% of gross monthly income
- HDB uses the actual loan interest rate for MSR assessment (no stress-test rate)

### Bank Loan for HDB Flat
- MSR applies at 30%
- Bank uses stress-test rate of 4.0% for MSR/TDSR assessment
- LTV limit is 75% (lower than HDB loan's 80%)

## Practical Impact of MSR

MSR significantly constrains the loan quantum for HDB flat buyers, particularly in high-price towns like Bishan, Queenstown, or Toa Payoh.

Example: 5-room resale flat in Queenstown priced at SGD 1,100,000
- 25% down payment: SGD 275,000
- Loan required: SGD 825,000
- Monthly repayment at 3.5% over 25 years: ~SGD 4,100
- Income needed to satisfy 30% MSR: SGD 4,100 / 0.30 = SGD 13,700/month minimum

## Historical Context
MSR was introduced in August 2013 at 30% for HDB flat purchases, extended to ECs in December 2013. The 30% threshold has remained unchanged since introduction.
