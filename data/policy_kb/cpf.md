# CPF Usage Rules for Property Purchase

CPF Ordinary Account (OA) funds can be used to purchase residential property in Singapore. Usage is subject to several limits and eligibility conditions.

## CPF Accounts Overview

- **Ordinary Account (OA)**: 2.5% per annum interest. Usable for property, investment, education.
- **Special Account (SA)**: 4.0% per annum interest. Locked for retirement — cannot be used for property.
- **MediSave**: Locked for healthcare — cannot be used for property.

All property-related CPF usage comes from the OA only.

## Properties Where CPF OA Can Be Used

### HDB Flats
- Fully eligible for HDB concessionary loan repayments
- Eligible for bank loan down payment and monthly instalments
- Subject to Valuation Limit (VL) and remaining lease conditions

### Private Residential Properties (Condos, Landed)
- Eligible for down payment and monthly mortgage instalments
- Subject to Valuation Limit and Withdrawal Limit
- Subject to remaining lease conditions

### Properties NOT Eligible for CPF Usage
- Commercial properties
- Industrial properties
- Properties with remaining lease below 20 years

## Key CPF Usage Limits

### Valuation Limit (VL)
Total CPF withdrawn for a property (by all owners) cannot exceed the lower of:
- Purchase price of the property
- Valuation at time of purchase

Once VL is reached, no more CPF OA can be withdrawn for that property.

### Withdrawal Limit (WL)
Total CPF withdrawn can exceed VL up to 120% of VL, but only if:
- The property's remaining lease covers the youngest owner to at least age 95

If remaining lease does NOT cover youngest owner to age 95:
- CPF usage is capped at the VL (no excess above VL)
- Further, if remaining lease is less than 30 years, CPF usage is prorated based on the ratio of (remaining lease / (95 - youngest owner's age))

## Remaining Lease and CPF — Detailed Rules

### Remaining Lease >= 30 Years AND Covers Youngest Owner to Age 95
- Full CPF usage up to Valuation Limit
- Withdrawal Limit (120% of VL) also available

### Remaining Lease >= 20 Years but Does NOT Cover Youngest Owner to Age 95
- CPF usage capped at prorated VL
- Prorated VL = VL × (remaining lease) / (95 - youngest owner's age)
- No access to Withdrawal Limit

### Remaining Lease < 20 Years
- CPF CANNOT be used at all for this property

## CPF Usage and Buyer Age — Example

Scenario: SC buyer, age 45, buying HDB flat with 55 years remaining lease
- Coverage check: 55 years remaining → buyer would be 45 + 55 = 100 at end of lease → covers to age 95 ✓
- Full CPF usage up to VL and WL

Scenario: SC buyer, age 50, buying HDB flat with 40 years remaining lease
- Coverage check: 40 years → buyer would be 50 + 40 = 90 at end of lease → does NOT cover to age 95
- CPF usage prorated: VL × 40 / (95 - 50) = VL × 40/45 = 88.9% of VL

Scenario: SC buyer, age 55, buying HDB flat with 15 years remaining lease
- Remaining lease < 20 years → NO CPF usage at all

## CPF OA as Opportunity Cost

Money in CPF OA earns a guaranteed 2.5% per annum (first SGD 20,000 earns 3.5% — Medisave top-up floor). Using CPF OA for property means:
- Forgoing the 2.5% guaranteed return
- Property must appreciate or yield rentally above 2.5% to justify CPF usage

CPF also accrues notional interest when withdrawn for property: when the property is sold, the CPF used plus accrued interest must be refunded to CPF OA before any cash profit is realised.

## CPF OA Accrued Interest on Sale

When property is sold:
- All CPF principal withdrawn must be refunded to OA
- Accrued interest at 2.5% per annum on the withdrawn amount must also be refunded
- Only the proceeds above (principal + accrued interest) represent cash profit
- This can significantly reduce apparent cash profit on sale

## CPF for ABSD and BSD

CPF OA can also be used to pay:
- Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD)
- Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD)
Subject to CPF withdrawal limits for the property.

## Foreign Buyers and CPF

Only Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents have CPF accounts. Foreigners cannot use CPF for property purchases — all payments must be in cash.

## CPF and HDB Loans

For HDB concessionary loans:
- No minimum cash down payment required (entire 20% down payment can be from CPF OA)
- Monthly instalments can be fully serviced from CPF OA
- CPF balance in OA must be sufficient; otherwise cash top-up is required
